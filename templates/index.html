About RetinaCare

RetinaCare uses AI-assisted retinal image analysis to help screen for diabetic retinopathy. The system provides a quick preliminary result that should be reviewed by a qualified medical professional.

Diabetic Retinopathy

Diabetic retinopathy is a diabetes-related eye condition that can affect the blood vessels of the retina. Regular eye screening can help with early detection.
